Bottom Line: Macro posture shows a market leaning toward firmer inflation and less benign policy expectations—with higher USD, rising yields, a bid in the energy complex and resilient gold all pointing to stickier inflation and tighter financial conditions—while sector leadership in defensives and real-asset exposures validates that tilt and the broad-based weakness in growth- and rate-sensitive equities underscores mounting concern about the durability of the growth impulse under higher-for-longer rates.
